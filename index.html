A Warning To The Young Man Against The Adulterous Woman

Proverbs 7:6-27

For at the window of my house, I looked through my casement, and beheld among the simple ones. I discerned among the youths, a young man void of understanding, passing through the street near her corner and he went the way to her house. In the twilight of the evening, in the black and dark night. And behold, there met him a woman in the attire of a harlot, and subtle of heart. She is loud and stubborn; her feet abide not in her house. Now she is outside, now in the streets, and lies in wait at every corner. So she caught him and kissed him. And with an impudent face said unto him, "I have peace offerings with me; this day have I paid my vows. Therefore came I forth to meet you, diligently to seek your face, and I have found you. I have decked my bed with coverings of tapestry, with colored spreads, with fine linen of Egypt. I have perfumed my bed with myrrh, aloes, and cinnamon. Come let us take our fill of love until the morning: let us delight ourselves with love, for my husband is not at home. He has gone on a long journey, he has taken a bag of money with him, and will come home at the day appointed." With her enticing speech she caused him to yield, with the flattering of her lips she seduced him. He goes after her immediately, as an ox goes to the slaughter, or as a fool to the correction of stocks; till an arrow strikes through his liver; as a bird hastens to the snare, and knows not that it is his life. Harken unto me now therefore, O you children, and attend to the words of my mouth. Let not your heart turn aside to her ways, go not astray in her paths. For she has cast down many wounded: yea, many strong men have been slain by her. For her house is the way to sheol, going down to the chambers of death.
Amen.

Meditation

An all-time favorite verse of mine. I find myself looking for this verse from time to time, whenever something happens around me and I remember that among Solomon's poems, there is a chapter that perfectly explains what I am experiencing. Wisdom is indeed priceless. It is always useful to know that whenever you feel lost, happy, or sad, you could always open the book of proverbs and you will find some guidance. A warning to the young naive man, against adultery. This is very applicable to our current age. With increased permissiveness. Prostitution has evolved and changed greatly with time. There is a reason it is the oldest known profession in the world, and illegal, looked down upon yet it can never dissapear from the face of this world Satan is after our souls. Protect your soul, nurture your soul, feed your soul. Caring for your soul is self-care. Your higher self is your godly self. Believe me when I tell you that you want a perfect relationship with God. When everything aligns in your life you will find yourself seeking God. And although this verse targets the young man, warns him, the woman should also take heed to not lose herself to harlotry. The harlot is insanely seductive that is the reason for her mass appeal. And I dare you that you could learn a lot from her. Yes. Even in the most wretched places you will sure be shocked to find value. Does not Jesus himself ask us to be as wise as the serpents - the being that brings temptation to humans in the Garden of Eden? Classic separation of the art from the artist. And yes, the harlot has a lot of men chasing after her love. Charm is irresistible. And useful everywhere, like in business and career. So, be wise. Young man, or woman.
